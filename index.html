Embrace

Chess where nobody gets captured.

Play the computer

To install: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Inspired by Paco Ŝako, the “peace chess” board game designed by Felix Albers. This is an unofficial fan-made game. If you enjoy it, please buy the original to support its creators. Its pieces really do lock together into pairs.

How to play

  1. It starts like chess. Same board, same pieces, same moves. But nothing is ever captured.
  2. Hug. Move onto a lone enemy piece and the two pieces become a pair on that square.
  3. Pairs are shared. On your turn you can move a pair by moving your piece in it, using that piece's move. Its partner comes along. Pairs only move to empty squares.
  4. Chain reactions. Move a lone piece onto a pair and it takes the place of your piece there. The piece that gets bumped out has to move right away. It can land on an empty square, hug a lone enemy, or bump into another pair and keep the chain going.
  5. Kings keep to themselves. A king only moves to empty squares. Castling works like chess. There is no check.
  6. Pawns step forward onto empty squares and move diagonally to hug or bump. A pawn that reaches the far side becomes a queen.
  7. To win, hug the enemy king. Any of your pieces will do, even one at the end of a chain. Keep your own king covered.
